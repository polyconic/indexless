Selected Works

/

  • On Distributed Communications Networks

    Paul Baran1962

    RAND's case for distributed mesh routing and "message blocks" — the survivability argument packet switching was built on.

  • Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System

    Satoshi Nakamoto2008

    Nine pages on using a proof-of-work chain to order transactions without a trusted third party.

  • Nikola Tesla Files, Part 3

    Federal Bureau of Investigation1957

    The Bureau's file on the custody and review of Tesla's papers following his death in 1943.

  • The TEXTFILES.COM Archive

    Various BBS authors, compiled by Jason Scott1998

    The written record of the BBS era — phreaking, zines, ANSI art, and the folklore of dial-up culture.

  • Geometric Overpass Extraction from Vector Road Data and DSMs

    Joshua Schpok2011

    Working out which roads pass over which, from flat vector road data plus a digital surface model.

  • OMNISEC: LLM-Driven Provenance-based Intrusion Detection

    Wenrui Cheng et al.2025

    Pointing a language model at system provenance graphs to flag intrusions without hand-written detection rules.

  • The ERTS-1 Investigation (ER-600)

    R. Bryan Erb1974

    NASA on the first Earth Resources Technology Satellite — the mission later renamed Landsat 1.

  • The Official U.S. Army Illustrated Guide to Edible Wild Plants

    U.S. Army2003

    Field identification of edible wild plants, illustrated, from the Army's survival curriculum.

  • Los Angeles Air Raid Sirens Archive

    Dennis H2006

    A hobbyist's survey of the city's Cold War civil-defense siren network: models, sites, and recordings.

  • Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA)

    Leonard Rosenthol et al.2020

    The spec for cryptographically signed provenance metadata bound to images, audio, and video.

  • Attention Is All You Need

    Ashish Vaswani et al.2017

    The Transformer: attention alone, no recurrence, no convolution. Most of the current field descends from this.

  • The Consequences of One's First Programming Language

    Richard L. Wexelblat1981

    An early look at how the first language you learn shapes the way you write every language after it.

  • Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces

    Albert Gu and Tri Dao2023

    Selective state space models — a linear-time architecture that stays competitive with attention at long context.

  • BitChat Protocol Whitepaper

    Permissionlesstech2025

    A Bluetooth mesh messaging protocol: no servers, no accounts, no phone numbers.

  • The Concept of the Ruliad

    Stephen Wolfram2021

    Wolfram's proposed limit object: the entangled result of running every possible computational rule.

  • Project Blue Book (UFO) Files, Part 1

    Federal Bureau of Investigation1977

    The Bureau's file on the Air Force's long-running UFO investigation.

  • Unison: Combinatorial Register Allocation and Instruction Scheduling

    Roberto Castañeda Lozano

    Treating register allocation and instruction scheduling as one combinatorial optimization problem instead of two heuristic passes.

  • Techniques for Program Verification

    Greg Nelson1980

    The thesis behind Simplify — congruence closure and the combination of decision procedures that SMT solvers still lean on.

  • End-to-End Arguments in System Design

    J. H. Saltzer, D. P. Reed and D. D. Clark1984

    Why a function usually belongs at the endpoints of a system rather than in the network beneath it.

  • E-graphs Research Community

    egraphs-good2021

    The hub for equality saturation — rewriting programs by keeping every equivalent version around at once.

  • An Introduction to Spectral Graph Theory

    Jiaqi Jiang2012

    Reading a graph's structure off the eigenvalues of its adjacency and Laplacian matrices.

  • BLAKE3 Cryptographic Hash Function Specification

    Jack O'Connor et al.2020

    A Merkle-tree hash built to be fast and parallel: the spec, the test vectors, and the design rationale.

  • Nested Learning: A New ML Paradigm for Continual Learning

    Ali Behrouz & Vahab Mirrokni2025

    Framing a model as nested optimization problems running at different update rates, aimed at learning without forgetting.
